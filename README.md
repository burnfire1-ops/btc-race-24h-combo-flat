COMBO-FLAT is a long-only dip-buying bot for the make.fun BTC Race 24h competition (cbBTC vs USDC, 1-minute bars, $1,000 paper). It enters on a 75 bps flush below the 90-minute SMA with 15-minute RSI(2) under 20, or on at least two of four 15-minute mean-reversion legs while the market is flat (15-minute ATR under 20 bps of price), and exits on a 3% take-profit, a 1% stop, a trailing stop that arms at +2%, or a 4-hour time stop. On the frozen 24-hour dev window it made a net PnL of +$15.90 on a single trade (trailing-stop exit), passing the dev gate.
